Welcome to Delizio Restaurant

Delizio is a workspace for managing the records behind daily restaurant service. Maintain menu categories, prices and availability; record orders and their quantities; and manage table status, waiter details and table assignments. The dashboard brings these records together and highlights today's sales from confirmed orders and confirmed-order history.

Set up your menu

Organize menu categories, prices, availability and individual menu items.

Record and review orders

Track each customer's order type, date, menu, amount and status, with quantities in order details.

Manage tables and waiters

Maintain table location, capacity and status, keep waiter details, and record waiter-to-table assignments.
